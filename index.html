Wake Windows — Infant Nap Schedule & Wake Windows Planner

Wake Windows is a free, evidence-checked infant nap-schedule and wake-window planner. Enter your baby's age, wake time, and wake windows and it builds a same-day nap plan with loose guidance windows — anywhere in a window counts, these are ranges, not deadlines. Under about 6 months (corrected age) an infant's circadian clock is still maturing, so sleepy cues beat clock times.

What is a wake window?

A wake window is the stretch of time a baby is awake between one sleep and the next. It grows with age: a newborn may manage well under two hours, while a toddler can stay up for several. Wake windows are a practice-based estimate, not a validated rule, so the planner treats them as a starting point and lets your baby's cues have the final say. See wake windows by age for the published ranges.

What it does

  • Generates a nap and bedtime schedule from wake windows and age.
  • Interactive sleep troubleshooter for early waking, short naps, and false starts / split nights.
  • Daylight-saving / timezone shift planner (ease the day over ~15 min/day).
  • Local-first sleep & nap log and a read-only sitter/grandparent share link.
  • No account, no AI, no data sold — everything stays on your device, one-click delete.

Safe sleep

Place baby fully on their back for every sleep, day and night, until age 1. Use a firm, flat, non-inclined surface; no soft bedding, pillows, or bumpers. Room-share without bed-sharing, ideally for the first 6 months.

Evidence tiers

Guidance is labelled Tier 1 (evidence-based consensus and peer-reviewed norms), Tier 2 (practice-based heuristic — "wake windows" are a useful estimate, not a validated rule), or Tier 3 (practitioner convention). Guidance last reviewed 2026-06-07.
